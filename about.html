1.Introduction and company overview

Rebuild You Skills Academy (Pty) Ltd, trading as Rebuild You Training Centre, is a South African-based skills development and training organisation committed to equipping individuals and organisations with practical, industry-relevant competencies required to succeed in today’s dynamic and competitive job market.

The company was established in response to the growing disconnect between formal education and workplace readiness, where many individuals possess qualifications but lack the practical skills, confidence and exposure required to secure and sustain employment. Rebuild You Skills Academy addresses this gap by delivering structured, hands-on training programmes that focus on real-world application, professional conduct and measurable outcomes.

Operating as a private company, Rebuild You Skills Academy provides revenue-generating training services to corporate clients, small and medium enterprises, and individuals seeking to improve their employability and professional capabilities. The organisation offers a range of programmes designed to develop both technical and soft skills, ensuring learners are not only qualified but also workplace-ready.

The company forms part of a broader dual-entity model, working alongside Rebuild You Training Centre (NPO), which focuses on community upliftment and skills development initiatives for unemployed and underserved individuals. While both entities share a unified vision of empowerment and transformation, they operate as separate legal structures with independent governance, financial systems and operational frameworks. This ensures full compliance with regulatory requirements while enabling both sustainability and impact.

Rebuild You Skills Academy adopts a practical, outcome-driven training approach that integrates skills development with employment readiness. Programmes are designed to simulate real workplace environments, enabling learners to gain confidence, develop competence and transition effectively into employment or entrepreneurial opportunities.

Through its commitment to excellence, integrity and empowerment, the company is positioned to become a trusted training provider, supporting workforce development and contributing to economic participation at both community and corporate levels.

3.Vision, mission and core values

To be a recognised leader in practical skills development and workforce readiness training in South Africa, delivering programmes that bridge the gap between education and employment, while empowering individuals to participate meaningfully in the economy.

Rebuild You Skills Academy envisions a future where individuals are not limited by lack of opportunity, but are equipped with the skills, confidence and professional mindset required to succeed in the workplace and contribute to sustainable economic growth.

Mission

To design and deliver high-quality, practical and industry-aligned training programmes that equip individuals and organisations with the competencies required for workplace success.

  • Providing accessible and structured training solutions

  • Enhancing employability and career readiness
  • Supporting organisations in building skilled and capable workforces
  • Creating pathways to employment, entrepreneurship and personal growth

Through a results-driven and hands-on approach, Rebuild You Skills Academy ensures that training is not only educational, but transformational.

11.Compliance and accreditation strategy

The company is committed to maintaining high standards of compliance and quality in all training programmes and operational processes. Quality. Integrity. Excellence.

Training is currently delivered as skills-based programmes with certificates of completion. These certificates validate participation and achievement, ensuring learners gain recognised proof of skills development.

Strategically positioned to pursue accreditation with

Services SETA

Services Sector Education and Training Authority. For business services, administration and customer service programmes.

CATHSSETA

Culture, Art, Tourism, Hospitality and Sport Sector Education and Training Authority. For hospitality, tourism, events and related skills programmes.

QCTO

Quality Council for Trades and Occupations. For occupational qualifications, skills development and artisan-related programmes.

Phased approach to accreditation

Gap analysis

Assess current systems, policies and compliance requirements.

System development

Develop and implement required policies, procedures and quality systems.

Staff capacity building

Ensure team competence and awareness of accreditation standards.

Internal monitoring

Conduct internal audits and reviews to ensure continuous compliance.

Preparation and submission

Compile and submit the accreditation application with supporting evidence.

Accreditation achieved

Achieve accreditation and maintain ongoing compliance and improvement.

A phased approach is followed to ensure full compliance and readiness before accreditation application.

13.Growth and expansion strategy

Short-term (0 to 12 months)

  • Deliver pilot training programmes to validate our model and impact
  • Develop a strong training portfolio focused on market needs
  • Build brand awareness and community trust

Medium-term (1 to 3 years)
